Performances
Samskrithi School of Dance

After completing their foundational training, Samskrithi students are invited to perform at temple festivals, cultural celebrations, competitions, ticketed productions, and more. Their performances are consistently praised by audiences and critics alike for their sincerity, discipline, and rich content. Arangetrams at Samskrithi are held to high artistic standards, reflecting years of rigorous training. Samskrithi has earned numerous accolades in both solo and group categories at dance competitions.





Arangetrams

Arangetram is the first formal full-length solo performance by the dancer after several years of arduous training with a teacher and an introduction to the Hindu philosophy and culture via Puranas, Ithihaasas and Carnatic music. Dances are presented in a sequence referred to as ‘Margam’ and are intended to showcase the dancer’s scholarship of the art, in presence of the teacher, connoisseurs of dance, family and friends. It signifies the beginning of a lifelong journey in dance.
