Our Mission

We aim to promote the rich heritage of Bharatanatyam by offering classical training that allows students to develop their skill, passion, and respect for the arts.

At Samskrithi School of Dance, we nurture a lifelong relationship with Bharatanatyam—one rooted in disciplined practice and reflective engagement. Through years of dedicated training and engagement with Indian classical arts, we invite each student and dancer to make Bharatanatyam a form of spiritual inquiry in their daily life. We strive to share with our community, too, through performances, classes and demonstrations.
