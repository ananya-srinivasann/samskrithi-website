Dance the Divine. Embody its Grace.

Bharatanatyam is a classical dance from Southern India. It was initially known as 'Sadir' and was an offering to the Hindu Gods as part of the daily temple rituals. Later, in the 20th century, it was renamed as 'Bharatanatyam' and moved from the temples to the proscenium stage. The dance also has references in the 2nd century Tamil epic Silappadikaram and is considered the oldest classical dance tradition of India.

Learn the art and live it. Through discipline practice and reflection, we invite each student and dancer to make Bharatanatyam a meaningful part of daily life.
